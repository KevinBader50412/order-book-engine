# Sample session for ./engine --replay examples/orders.txt
# Prices are decimal with at most two places; they are stored as integer ticks.

# Build up both sides of the book
ADD 1 SELL LIMIT 50 101.00
ADD 2 SELL LIMIT 30 101.50
ADD 3 SELL LIMIT 20 101.00
ADD 4 BUY LIMIT 40 100.00
ADD 5 BUY LIMIT 25 99.50
PRINT

# Crosses the spread: fills 50 from order 1, then 20 from order 3 (same
# price, later arrival), then 10 at 101.50 from order 2
ADD 6 BUY LIMIT 80 101.50
PRINT

# Market sell sweeps the bids; 15 is left over and is cancelled
ADD 7 SELL MARKET 80

# Cancels: one resting order, one unknown id, one already filled
CANCEL 2
CANCEL 99
CANCEL 1

# Rejections
ADD 8 BUY LIMIT 0 100.00
ADD 9 BUY LIMIT 10 0
ADD 6 BUY LIMIT 10 100.00

ADD 10 BUY LIMIT 15 100.25
ADD 11 SELL LIMIT 15 100.75
PRINT
QUIT
